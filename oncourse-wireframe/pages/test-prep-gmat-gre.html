Test Snapshot

Test Snapshot: GMAT & GRE

Format, sections, timing and scoring for each test.

GMAT Focus Edition [Verify against mba.com (GMAC) before publishing]

GMAT Focus at a glance

Format
Computer-adaptive (question level), at a test centre or online. Sections can be taken in any order; up to 3 answers per section can be changed.
Sections
Quantitative Reasoning (21 questions), Verbal Reasoning (23 questions), Data Insights (20 questions).
Timing
2 hours 15 minutes: 45 minutes per section, with one optional 10 minute break.
Scoring
Total 205 to 805. Each section scored 60 to 90. No essay.
GRE General Test [Verify against ets.org before publishing]

GRE at a glance

Format
Section-level adaptive, at a test centre or at home. The second Verbal and Quant sections adapt to performance in the first.
Sections
Analytical Writing (1 task), Verbal Reasoning (2 sections, 27 questions), Quantitative Reasoning (2 sections, 27 questions).
Timing
About 1 hour 58 minutes: Writing 30 min, Verbal 41 min, Quant 47 min. No scheduled break.
Scoring
Verbal and Quant each 130 to 170. Analytical Writing 0 to 6. On-screen calculator provided for Quant.

GMAT vs GRE: Which Test Fits?

A concise comparison to help decide which test to prepare for.

GMAT FocusGRE
Accepted byPrimarily business schools (MBA, specialised masters)Business schools and most graduate programmes (MS, PhD, some law)
FormatQuestion-adaptive; 3 sections in any orderSection-adaptive; Writing first, then Verbal and Quant
Length2 h 15 minAbout 1 h 58 min
EssayNoneOne Analytical Writing task
QuantNo calculator; data sufficiency now in Data InsightsOn-screen calculator provided
VerbalReading comprehension and critical reasoning; no vocabulary-based questionsText completion and sentence equivalence rely on vocabulary
Scoring205 to 805 total130 to 170 per section, Writing 0 to 6
May suit candidates whoAre applying only to business school and are strong in data analysisAre weighing MBA and other masters programmes, or prefer a calculator in Quant

Most business schools accept either test. Check each programme's policy, and take a diagnostic of both before choosing.
