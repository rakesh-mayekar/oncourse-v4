Our Journey

A Vision That Began in 2010. A Commitment That Continues.

Founded in 2010, OnCourse has grown from an admissions consultancy into a comprehensive education guidance partner. Our experienced mentors and senior counsellors, graduates of institutions including Harvard, Northwestern, NYU Stern, LSE, King’s College London, SMU, Ashoka University, among others, bring valuable academic and professional perspectives to the journeys we guide.

🎯

Our Mission

To help students discover their potential and pursue meaningful educational opportunities through bespoke guidance and experienced mentorship.

Unlocking Individual Potential
🧭

Our Philosophy

Every student has a different story. We believe the best guidance begins with understanding the individual, not fitting them into a formula.

The People Behind OnCourse

Experienced Minds. Bespoke Guidance.

At the heart of OnCourse is a team of experienced mentors and senior counsellors who bring depth of knowledge, considered perspectives and a genuine commitment to each student’s journey.

With backgrounds shaped by leading academic institutions and diverse professional experiences, our team understands that meaningful guidance goes beyond knowing the admissions process. It requires the ability to recognise individual strengths, ask the right questions and develop a bespoke strategy around each student’s ambitions.

Led by our co-founders and senior leadership, we continue to build on the experience and philosophy that have shaped OnCourse since 2010.

General Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

General questions about how OnCourse works. For programme-specific questions, use the Know more links below.

Unlike traditional commission-based agencies representing specific universities, OnCourse is a purely fiduciary advisory firm representing the student. We do not receive referral fees from universities; our sole objective is maximizing student admissions into the world's most selective institutions.
Students are paired based on their target academic discipline, geographic ambitions, and psychological learning style. A prospective computer science major targeting MIT or Stanford works directly with mentors with STEM admissions backgrounds, while an MBA candidate works with top M7 alumni.
For undergraduate admissions, ideal engagement begins in Class 8, 9, or 10. Early engagement allows authentic development of extracurricular spikes, academic course selection (IB/AP/A-Levels), and research publications without senior-year stress. We also offer fast-track application packaging for Class 12 and MBA candidates.
Yes, all our offices in Mumbai, New Delhi, Gurgaon, and Dubai welcome families by appointment. We also maintain secure high-definition virtual counseling rooms for international families worldwide.
