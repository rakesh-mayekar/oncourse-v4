University Transfer Admissions Advisory

Strategic transfer counsel for university students transitioning to Top 30 US and global institutions. [Transfer pathway criteria and requirements to be finalized with client]

Strategic Methodology

The 3-Pillar University Transfer Process

01

Curriculum & Credit Audit

Forensic evaluation of course syllabus equivalencies, general education requirements, and transferable credits across target institutions to prevent graduation delays.

Deliverable: Credit Transfer Matrix
02

Faculty Mentorship Strategy

Structured protocols to secure powerful collegiate letters of recommendation from current university professors, TAs, and research lab leads.

Deliverable: Professor Briefing Packs
03

Institutional Fit Narrative

Crafting a compelling "Why Transfer" essay that frames your academic progression positively—demonstrating why your current institution no longer satisfies your intellectual trajectory.

Deliverable: Tailored Personal Essays
